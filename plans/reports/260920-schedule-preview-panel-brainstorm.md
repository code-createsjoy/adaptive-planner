## Ideas Explored

### 1. Tooltip / Hover Preview
Dismissed — useless on mobile/touch, hard to read rich content.

### 2. Modal / Dialog Preview
Dismissed — too disruptive, user loses sight of other options.

### 3. Accordion / Expand-under

### 4. Persistent Side Panel (Chosen)
Options list 40% left + persistent preview panel 60% right.
Click any option → right panel swaps content instantly.
Desktop: side-by-side. Mobile: stacked.
